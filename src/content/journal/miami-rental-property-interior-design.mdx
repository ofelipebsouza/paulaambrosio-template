Design for the Photograph
The first visit to your property happens on a screen. Every room needs one strong, clear composition: a
beautiful sofa facing the view, a bed with a striking headboard, a dining table styled for a moment. We
design with the listing photos in mind, making sure each space has a focal point that stops the scroll.
Good natural light and a cohesive palette make the whole set of images feel like one story.

Durability Without Looking Durable
Rental furniture works harder than furniture in a private home. The secret is to choose pieces that look
refined but are built for heavy use.

    •   Performance fabrics on sofas and chairs that clean easily and resist sun.
    •   Porcelain and quartz surfaces that resist stains and scratches.
    •   Solid construction and commercial grade hardware on cabinets and doors.
    •   Washable slipcovers and white bedding that can be bleached and replaced easily.

This is where our hospitality experience makes a real difference. Hotels have solved these problems for
decades.

Comfort Guests Remember
Reviews are rarely about the chandelier. They are about sleeping well, finding everything they need and
feeling cared for. We invest in excellent mattresses and pillows, blackout shades in the bedrooms,
strong Wi Fi, generous towels, a well equipped kitchen and a clear place to unpack. Small touches such
as a welcome guide, beach towels and chairs, and a coffee station earn words like thoughtful and
perfect in reviews.

A Sense of Place
Guests come to Miami for Miami. A rental that could be anywhere misses an opportunity. We bring in
the light, colors and materials of the coast, local art or photography, and a relaxed tropical elegance that
tells guests exactly where they are.

Smart Operations
Design should also make management easier. Keyless entry, smart thermostats, leak sensors, locked
owner storage, durable finishes that are easy to clean and a detailed inventory of every item help
cleaners and managers work quickly and protect your investment. Before designing, always review city
and building rules on rentals, since many Miami condominiums have their own restrictions.

Design as a Return on Investment
A professionally designed rental can support higher nightly rates, better occupancy and stronger
