Planning early saves time, money and a lot of demolition.

Understand What the Developer Delivers
Every building has its own standard of delivery. Some units arrive fully finished with flooring, kitchen and
bathrooms. Others are delivered with a more basic package, and some leave floors and certain finishes
to the owner. The first step is to read the specifications carefully and understand exactly what is
included, which options exist and the deadlines for choosing them.

Upgrades Worth Considering
Not every upgrade adds value. The ones we usually recommend are those that are difficult or expensive
to change later.

    •   Electrical and lighting points. Additional recessed lights, outlets in the right places and wiring
        for shades and automation are simple before the ceilings close and complicated after.
    •   Flooring. If the developer offers a floor you love, installing it before closing avoids moving
        furniture and managing building rules later.
    •   Kitchen and bath layout. Small changes in plumbing or cabinetry are far easier during
        construction.
    •   Blocking in walls for heavy art, TVs and floating vanities.

Purely decorative choices, such as some light fixtures or hardware, are often better handled later in the
interior design phase.

Design Before You Receive the Keys
The best moment to hire an interior designer is before closing, not after. With the floor plans in hand,
we can develop the full layout, furniture plan, lighting and millwork. We use 3D renders so clients can
see their home before it exists, and we order furniture with long lead times in advance. When the unit is
delivered, installation can begin almost immediately.

Know the Building Rules
Condominium associations set rules for renovations, contractors, insurance, working hours and elevator
use. New buildings often have additional restrictions in the first months after delivery, when many
owners are moving in at the same time. We plan schedules around these rules from the start, as we
explain in Navigating Luxury High Rise Condominium Renovations.
