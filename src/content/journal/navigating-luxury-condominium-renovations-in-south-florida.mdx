Designing within world-renowned towers such as Oceana Bal Harbour or Regalia Sunny Isles demands far more than surface decorating. It requires precision interior architecture.

## The technical layer

From managing sound transmission classes (STC ratings) under custom hardwood floors to engineering drop ceiling coves that bypass rigid concrete structural slabs, our studio navigates every technical nuance. Early collaboration with building management and licensed trade specialists ensures smooth execution without costly delays or HOA disruptions.

## What owners should prepare

Condominium boards will ask for contractor licensing, certificates of insurance, acoustic membrane specifications and strict adherence to construction hours. Preparing this package before demolition begins protects both the schedule and the neighbors.

If you own a residence in [Bal Harbour](/locations/bal-harbour) or [Aventura](/locations/aventura), our [residential interior design practice](/services/luxury-residential-interior-design-miami) manages this process end to end.
