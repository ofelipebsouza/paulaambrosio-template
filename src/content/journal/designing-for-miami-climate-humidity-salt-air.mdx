Late September is the heart of hurricane season in South Florida, and it is the moment every
homeowner remembers that Miami is not only a lifestyle. It is a climate. Humidity, salt air, intense sun
and summer storms are part of daily life here, and a beautiful interior must be designed to live with all
of them.

After more than two decades designing homes, and more than a decade in Miami, we have learned that
the most elegant interiors are the ones that still look new five years later. That longevity is never an
accident. It starts with the brief.

Why Climate Is the First Brief in Miami
In many cities, material selection begins with color and texture. In Miami, it begins with a question: how
will this behave at 80 percent humidity, a few blocks from the ocean, under the Florida sun?

A sofa fabric that is perfect in New York can fade in one season next to a west facing window in Sunny
Isles. A solid wood floor that performs beautifully in a dry climate can cup and move here. Brass that
looks warm and rich on the first day can spot and darken on a Bal Harbour balcony. Designing for the
climate protects your investment and saves you from replacing pieces that should have lasted a decade.

Humidity: The Invisible Designer
Humidity touches almost every decision inside a Miami home.

    •   Wood floors and millwork. We favor engineered wood floors and stable species such as white
        oak, and we let materials acclimate on site before installation. Millwork receives sealed finishes
        on every face, including the back panels most people never see.
    •   Air and circulation. A well designed home works together with its air conditioning. Closets, wine
        storage and laundry rooms need ventilation or dedicated dehumidification, especially in homes
        that stay closed while owners travel.
    •   Walls and wet areas. In bathrooms and laundry rooms we specify moisture resistant boards and
        finishes, and we choose paints made for humid environments. For texture, lime plaster and
        mineral finishes breathe beautifully in this climate.

Salt Air and Oceanfront Living
For oceanfront condominiums in Miami Beach, Surfside, Bal Harbour and Sunny Isles, salt is the quiet
enemy of metal and fabric.

    •   Hardware and fixtures. Outdoors and near open windows, we specify marine grade stainless
        steel, powder coated aluminum and finishes rated for coastal exposure. Unlacquered brass is
        gorgeous, but we reserve it for protected interior areas where its patina is welcome.
    •   Fabrics. Solution dyed acrylics and high quality performance fabrics resist fading, moisture and
        salt, and today they feel as soft as natural fibers. Pure linen and silk still have a place, but we
        position them away from direct sun and pair them with protective window treatments.
    •   Sun and UV. Intense light fades rugs, art and upholstery. Layered window treatments, UV film
        on glass and thoughtful furniture placement keep a room luminous without sacrificing what is
        inside it. You can read more in our guide to window treatments in Miami interior design.

Materials That Age Beautifully Here
Some materials seem made for South Florida. Porcelain slabs and large format porcelain tiles resist
moisture and stains with almost no maintenance. Sealed travertine and quartzite bring warmth and a
natural feel to floors and counters, and they stay cool underfoot. Outdoors, teak and powder coated
aluminum weather gracefully and need very little care.

Choosing stone is its own art, and we explain our approach in Timeless Stone Selection in Miami Interior
Design.

Designing With Hurricane Season in Mind
Storm season should shape a design, not interrupt it.

    •   Impact glass as an opportunity. Impact rated windows and doors are standard in most new and
        renovated South Florida homes. They allow generous openings to the view, and we plan curtain
        tracks, shades and millwork around their thicker frames from the first drawing.
    •   A plan for outdoor furniture. Terraces and balconies need pieces that can be secured or brought
        indoors before a storm. We design outdoor layouts with a clear storage plan and follow building
        guidance for storm warnings.
    •   Technology for owners who travel. Water leak sensors, humidity monitoring, backup power and
        remote access let owners check on their home from anywhere in the world. We cover this in
        Smart Homes in Miami.
    •   A complete inventory. Every turnkey project we deliver includes a detailed record of furniture,
        finishes and suppliers. It is useful for maintenance, and it is invaluable for insurance.

A Home That Stays Beautiful While You Are Away
Many of our clients live between Miami and another country. Their homes may stay closed for weeks
during the summer and the storm season. For them, climate aware design is not a detail. It is peace of
mind.

Through our turnkey interior design service, we select every material with the Miami climate in mind
and deliver each home with a simple care guide, so it is ready to welcome its owners whenever they
arrive.
