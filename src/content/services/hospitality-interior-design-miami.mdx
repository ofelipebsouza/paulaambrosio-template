We evaluate hospitality through the guest journey: the arrival sequence, acoustic transitions, ambient lighting gradients, tactile material warmth, brand identity, and operational efficiency. We create commercial spaces that feel as warm and considered as a private residence while exceeding contract durability and building codes.

## What the service includes

- **Boutique hotel and suite concept design** — signature design narratives for guest rooms, suites, and arrival lounges.
- **Cocktail lounges and member clubs** — custom bar architecture, intimate alcoves, acoustic baffling, layered mood lighting.
- **Wellness spas and beauty destinations** — hydrotherapy suites, treatment rooms, natural stone finishes, sensory environments.
- **Commercial FF&E procurement and custom fabrication** — contract-grade materials, treated luxury textiles, durable millwork.
- **Operational flow and code compliance** — staff pathways, ADA accessibility, life-safety clearances, durability standards.

## The process

1. **Brand narrative and guest journey** — positioning, target guest, and emotional touchpoints.
2. **Public and private space planning** — efficient layouts for check-in lounges, bar operations, and suites.
3. **Material and lighting engineering** — commercial-rated finishes, acoustic treatments, programmable lighting scenes.
4. **Contract FF&E procurement** — manufacturer orders, hospitality quantity pricing, testing certifications.
5. **Installation and launch styling** — turnover with operations, art positioning, and launch staging.

## Who we work with

Boutique hotel developers, hospitality operators, wellness brands, and commercial developers across Miami and South Florida — in direct coordination with licensed general contractors, MEP engineers, and brand operations teams.
