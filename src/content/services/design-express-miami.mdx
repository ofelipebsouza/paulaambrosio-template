Design Express exists for a specific situation: the residence is there, or it is about to be, and you want it finished, not after eighteen months of decisions but on a schedule you can plan your life around. Everything that makes our turnkey work is still here. What changes is how fast the decisions are made.

## The Promise, Stated Plain

A fully furnished, move-in-ready home in as little as 40 days. You arrive, the furniture is placed, the artwork is hung, the beds are dressed, the lamps are lit, the table is set. Nothing is on order. Nothing is waiting on you.

## What Is Included

- Design direction and space planning: one cohesive concept for the residence, with furniture plans and circulation resolved before anything is purchased.
- Material and finish specification: the palette for walls, floors, stone, cabinetry and hardware, documented so nothing is decided twice.
- 3D visualization where it earns its place: proportion, light and material reviewed in advance on the rooms where the risk is highest.
- Furniture, lighting and rug selection: trade-only pieces, considered as a composition rather than a shopping list.
- Procurement and vendor coordination: one purchase order trail, one point of contact, no showroom weekends on your side.
- Logistics and receiving: freight, inspection and consolidation in a secure climate-controlled warehouse.
- White-glove installation: delivery, assembly, placement and protection managed as a single coordinated day.
- Artwork, accessories and final styling: the layer that makes a photographed room feel like a home.

## How the Sequence Runs

1. Brief. A private discovery session to establish how you live, what the residence must do, and the date you need it ready.
2. Direction. Concept, palette and furniture plan presented together so approvals happen in one pass rather than five.
3. Specification. Every item selected, priced and confirmed. You see the full picture before a single order is placed.
4. Procurement. Orders are released, tracked and consolidated. While pieces are in transit, finishes and lighting are coordinated on site.
5. Installation. A scheduled white-glove day: furniture, rugs, artwork, window treatments and lighting set and adjusted.
6. Handover. Styling complete, walkthrough done, residence ready to live in.

## Who It Is For

- You have purchased in Miami and need the residence ready before a specific date: a season, a family visit, a closing on another property.
- You own a second home that is furnished but no longer finished, and you want it resolved rather than gradually improved.
- You are an international or out-of-state buyer who cannot be present for months of decisions and needs one studio to absorb them.
- You are a developer or investor delivering a show residence or a staged property on a fixed marketing calendar.
- You appreciate good design and simply do not want the project to become your second job.

## What You Receive

A resolved residence, photographed and ready to hand over: rooms composed rather than filled, every item received and inspected, documentation of what was specified, and a studio that remains reachable after the keys are in your hand.
