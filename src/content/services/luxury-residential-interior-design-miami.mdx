We emphasize bespoke rather than formulaic design: proportion, natural materials, craftsmanship, light, comfort, and an organic relationship between architecture and furniture. We believe luxury is not about excess, but about the quiet harmony between space, material, and everyday human rituals.

## What the service includes

- **Space planning and interior architecture**: spatial flow, sightlines, transitions, and ceiling volumes.
- **Materials and surface selection**: natural stones, artisanal plasters, fine hardwoods, and architectural metals.
- **Kitchens and bathrooms**: custom culinary centers, spa-inspired primary sanctuaries, and bespoke vanities.
- **Custom millwork and cabinetry**: library walls, wine displays, entertainment elevations, and dressing rooms.
- **Architectural and decorative lighting**: layered illumination, recessed cove details, and curated fixtures.
- **Furniture selection and bespoke pieces**: custom upholstery, artisan wood and stone tables, curated trade-only pieces.
- **Fine art and object curation**: acquisition advisory, gallery lighting integration, and sculpture plinths.
- **Procurement and purchasing management**: global orders, freight, warehouse receiving, white-glove delivery.
- **Final styling and accessorizing**: artisanal ceramics, textiles, tabletop objects, and sensory ambiance.
- **Project coordination**: close synergy with licensed general contractors, architects, and specialty trades.

## The process

1. **Discovery and programming**: exploring your lifestyle, daily routines, functional needs, and aesthetic aspirations.
2. **Concept and interior architecture**: schematic spatial plans, palette boards, and material samples.
3. **Design development and 3D previews**: millwork details, finish selections, and rendered spaces.
4. **Procurement and trade coordination**: furnishings orders, custom artisan fabrications, delivery tracking.
5. **Installation and final styling**: white-glove installation, art placement, accessory styling, and handover.

## Property types

Luxury homes, waterfront residences, condominiums, penthouses, complete renovations, and new construction throughout Greater Miami and South Florida.
