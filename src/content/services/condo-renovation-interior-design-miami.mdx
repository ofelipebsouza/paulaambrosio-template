## Define what should change

We start with the rooms that cause friction. In a kitchen, that might be a narrow route past the island. In a bathroom, it might be poor lighting or insufficient counter space. We document what should stay before deciding where a new layout, cabinetry or finish is worth the disruption.

## Review the condo requirements early

Ask management for its renovation application, work hours, insurance requirements and any restrictions on flooring, plumbing and trade access. Your licensed contractor confirms permits, construction methods and site conditions. We coordinate our design and specifications with the team so product selections are compatible with those decisions.

## Plan delivery before placing orders

Check service elevator dimensions, loading rules and the route into each room. We use those constraints to plan what can arrive assembled and what needs installation in the unit. If you live elsewhere, you can review layouts and materials remotely while the team confirms dimensions and access.
