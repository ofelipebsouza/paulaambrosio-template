Turnkey means complete. The furniture is installed, the artwork is placed, the lighting considered, the accessories styled. The rooms feel finished. Our role is to transform the design vision into a fully realized home, one accountable studio managing every layer between concept and moving day.

## Who the service is for

The service is positioned specifically for second homes, vacation properties, and international clients who need a structured local design team while participating in major decisions remotely. If you are acquiring a residence in Miami while living in New York, São Paulo, London or Madrid, the turnkey model replaces hundreds of local decisions with a single, coordinated process.

## What the service includes

- Interior design and space planning: comprehensive architectural layouts, furniture plans, and circulation optimization.
- 3D photorealistic visualization: experience materials, lighting, and custom millwork before anything is built.
- Materials, kitchens and bathrooms: natural stones, cabinetry, plumbing selections, and architectural surface finishes.
- Architectural and decorative lighting: layered lighting design, custom fixtures, and smart lighting scene coordination.
- Custom millwork and custom furniture: built ins, wall paneling, banquettes, and unique pieces.
- Furniture selection and procurement: designer pieces available through the trade, purchasing management, and warehouse consolidation.
- Vendor and project coordination: collaboration with licensed contractors and specialized trade fabrication timelines.
- Artwork, rugs and window treatments: fine art selection, custom hand-knotted rugs, and motorized acoustic drapery.
- Accessories and tabletop styling: decorative objects, handmade vessels, tabletop settings, and tailored home fragrances.
- Installation coordination and final styling: delivery oversight, artwork hanging, bed dressing, and the reveal.

## Our approach

Our turnkey method pairs European design sensibility with rigorous project coordination. We select each material and piece of furniture for the way it will be used, the quality of its finish and its delivery schedule. All items are received, inspected, and consolidated in a warehouse with climate control until the coordinated installation.

## The process

1. Understand: comprehensive discovery of your aesthetic vision, functional priorities, and lifestyle requirements.
2. Design: spatial plans, color palettes, custom millwork concepts, and finish palettes.
3. Visualize: detailed 3D renderings to review proportions, textures, and lighting in advance.
4. Source: custom furniture, natural stone, lighting, artisan rugs, and accessories.
5. Coordinate: warehouse consolidation, freight logistics, and trade contractor coordination.
6. Install: coordinated delivery, millwork placement, and protective on site assembly.
7. Style: artwork, luxury linens, decorative objects, and lighting adjustment.
8. Reveal: handover of your turnkey residence, illuminated and completely ready to live in.
