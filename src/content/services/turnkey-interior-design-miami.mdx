Turnkey means complete. The furniture is installed. The artwork is placed. The lighting has been considered. The accessories are styled. The rooms feel finished. Our role is to transform the design vision into a fully realized home — one accountable studio managing every layer between concept and move-in day.

## Who the service is for

The service is positioned specifically for second homes, vacation properties, and international clients who need a structured local design team while participating in major decisions remotely. If you are acquiring a residence in Miami while living in New York, São Paulo, London or Madrid, the turnkey model replaces hundreds of local decisions with a single, coordinated process.

## What the service includes

- **Interior design and space planning** — comprehensive architectural layouts, furniture plans, and circulation optimization.
- **3D photorealistic visualization** — experience materials, lighting, and custom millwork before anything is built.
- **Materials, kitchens and bathrooms** — natural stones, cabinetry, plumbing selections, and architectural surface finishes.
- **Architectural and decorative lighting** — layered lighting design, custom fixtures, and smart lighting scene coordination.
- **Custom millwork and custom furniture** — bespoke built-ins, wall paneling, banquettes, and one-of-a-kind pieces.
- **Furniture selection and procurement** — trade-only designer pieces, purchasing management, and warehouse consolidation.
- **Vendor and project coordination** — collaboration with licensed contractors and specialized trade fabrication timelines.
- **Artwork, rugs and window treatments** — fine art curation, custom hand-knotted rugs, and motorized acoustic drapery.
- **Accessories and tabletop styling** — decorative objects, bespoke vessels, tabletop settings, and tailored home fragrances.
- **Installation coordination and final styling** — white-glove delivery oversight, artwork hanging, bed dressing, and the reveal.

## Our approach

Our turnkey methodology unites meticulous European design vision with rigorous project coordination. We maintain direct relationships with master ateliers across Italy, France, and North America, ensuring every piece meets the highest standards of luxury and durability. All items are received, inspected, and consolidated in a climate-controlled warehouse until the coordinated white-glove installation.

## The process

1. **Understand** — comprehensive discovery of your aesthetic vision, functional priorities, and lifestyle requirements.
2. **Design** — spatial plans, color palettes, custom millwork concepts, and finish palettes.
3. **Visualize** — high-definition 3D renderings to review proportions, textures, and lighting in advance.
4. **Source** — bespoke furniture, natural stone, lighting, artisan rugs, and accessories.
5. **Coordinate** — warehouse consolidation, freight logistics, and trade contractor coordination.
6. **Install** — white-glove delivery, millwork placement, and protective on-site assembly.
7. **Style** — artwork, luxury linens, decorative objects, and lighting adjustment.
8. **Reveal** — handover of your turnkey residence, illuminated and completely move-in ready.
