A consultation is not a sales pitch. It is a working session where we sit together, review your space, discuss your priorities, and begin mapping the path from where you are to where you want to be.

## What to Expect

### Site Analysis and Creative Discovery

We evaluate your property's architectural potential, natural light, spatial flow, and the challenges that will shape the design direction. Whether we are walking through the space in person or reviewing floor plans and video tours remotely, the goal is the same: understand what the architecture gives us and what the design needs to solve.

### Lifestyle Assessment

Design is personal. Before we select a single material or sketch a single layout, we need to understand how you live. How you use your kitchen. Where you unwind. Whether you entertain often or prefer quiet evenings at home. This conversation shapes every decision that follows.

### Investment Roadmap

We provide clear, honest guidance on project scope, realistic timelines, and how to allocate your design budget for maximum impact. No surprises. No vague estimates. A transparent conversation about what it takes to deliver the home you are envisioning.

## Who This Is For

- You are acquiring a property in Miami and want expert guidance before closing.
- You have a home that no longer reflects how you live and are ready for a transformation.
- You are considering a renovation and need to understand the full scope before committing.
- You want a single studio to manage everything from concept to move-in day.

## How It Works

1. **Reach out** through our contact form or call the studio directly.
2. **We schedule** a private session at your convenience, in person or over video.
3. **We listen**, review your space, and ask the right questions.
4. **You receive** a clear assessment of scope, timeline, and next steps.

## After the Consultation

If we are the right fit, we move into the design phase: concept development, material selection, 3D visualization, procurement, installation, and final styling. One studio. One process. One accountable team from first conversation to final reveal.
