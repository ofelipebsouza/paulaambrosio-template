Miami architecture offers extraordinary possibilities: waterfront homes filled with natural light, sophisticated high-rise residences, contemporary estates and international second homes. Our approach begins by understanding how our clients live.

## The Miami residential landscape

Each Miami neighborhood asks something different of an interior:

- **Brickell and Edgewater** — vertical residences where panoramic glazing, acoustic comfort and glare control shape every material decision.
- **Coconut Grove** — lush, low-slung homes where indoor-outdoor flow and garden sightlines lead the plan.
- **Coral Gables** — historic Mediterranean proportion met with contemporary comfort and infrastructure.
- **Key Biscayne** — family-oriented waterfront living built around salt air, sun and durable natural materials.

## How we work in Miami

We manage the complete interior — space planning, interior architecture, custom millwork, lighting, furniture, art and styling — while coordinating directly with your architect and licensed general contractor. For clients abroad, the full process runs remotely: digital presentations, video walkthroughs and milestone reporting, ending in a coordinated white-glove installation.
