## A local studio for the way you live

From our office at 1938 NE 149th St, we help homeowners plan everything from one furnished living room to a complete interior. We begin with how you live: where people gather, what needs storage and which rooms you want ready first. Then we work through layout, finishes, furniture and installation in an order that suits your project.

## See a completed North Miami residence

At [Warm Modern Residence](/projects/home-aa/), oak panels, softly lit cabinetry and veined stone connect the living room, entry and kitchen. Instead of giving each space a different finish, we used a consistent grain and palette. A low ledge in the living room makes space for objects and art, while a floating console at the entry provides a landing place without interrupting circulation. The kitchen and bar show how lighting and a small number of materials can give each room its own character.

## Furnish your condo or plan a renovation

For homes that are ready for furniture, [Design Express](/design-express-miami/) covers room planning, furniture selection, delivery coordination and styling. If walls, cabinetry, lighting or finishes need to change, our [condo renovation service](/condo-renovation-interior-design-miami/) helps define the scope before you commit. Licensed contractors handle construction and permitting as applicable.
