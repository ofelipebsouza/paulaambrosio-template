## What to keep when you renovate an oceanfront condo

A better interior does not always start with a demolition plan. We walk through the home with an eye to what is already working: good stone floors, solid doors, useful cabinetry and design details that feel right for the building. We then identify the places where a targeted change could make a bigger difference, such as a kitchen that is hard to use, a living room with glare or a closet that does not make good use of wall height.

When the scope includes work in kitchens, baths or ceilings, we review the existing layout and design around what can be verified. Your independent licensed contractor handles construction. We make finish and furniture decisions in the context of that scope so you do not order a piece that depends on an unresolved dimension.

## Plan around access and delivery

A condo can look ready long before it is available for measurement or installation. Ask management for current delivery hours, elevator reservations and any requirements for trade access or corridor protection. Before ordering large pieces, we ask about the route from receiving to the residence. When an item will not fit the elevator or a tight turn, the answer may be a piece that assembles in the room, not a last minute delivery change.

If you are buying from abroad, we can review the floor plan and start the design conversation remotely. We need confirmed access, field measurements and product availability before committing to installation dates. Owners who spend part of the year here can share travel plans early so that design reviews and deliveries do not compete with their time at home.

## Choose the right service before choosing furniture

A finished condo with an interior that simply needs furniture may suit [Design Express](/design-express-miami/). A property that needs construction, new cabinetry and a full furniture plan calls for a more detailed [renovation scope](/condo-renovation-interior-design-miami/). If your goal is one point of coordination through installation, ask about [turnkey interiors](/turnkey-interior-design-miami/).

Share a floor plan, your closing or occupancy window and the rooms you want to address first. That gives us enough context to recommend a scope without assuming that every condo needs the same work.
