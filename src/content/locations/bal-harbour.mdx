Bal Harbour residences have their own rhythm: oceanfront light, generous terraces and room to slow down. We design interiors that feel calm and comfortable, with close attention to materials and how you use each room.

## Oceanfront condos and penthouses

We design for the view and the climate, choosing performance fabrics, natural stone, warm woods and lighting that works from sunrise to evening. [Custom millwork](/millwork/) brings storage and space for display without crowding the view.

## From concept to installation

Our [turnkey interior design service](/turnkey-interior-design-miami/) covers design and 3D presentations, procurement, installation and styling. We coordinate with your building and contractors, so you know what is needed at each stage.
