## A family home that works on an ordinary day

We start with the moments that repeat: coming home with bags, making breakfast, working from home and hosting family. That gives us a more useful brief than a style label. It tells us where to concentrate storage, where to create quieter rooms and how to keep a good connection to the garden.

## Coordinate the renovation before selecting finishes

In a whole-home renovation, millwork, lighting and furniture need to agree with the electrical and architectural plans. We coordinate those decisions with the project team so details such as outlet locations, door swings and the depth of built-ins are resolved early.
