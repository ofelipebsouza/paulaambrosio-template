## Materials that make sense by the ocean

At the coast, finish choices need to do more than look good in a sample. We consider glare, salt air, maintenance and how a room is used before specifying window coverings, surfaces and upholstery. The goal is a home that feels calm without being delicate.

## Make every inch of a condominium count

An entry bench, integrated pantry or tailored media wall can add storage without making a room feel smaller. We design [custom millwork](/millwork/) alongside the furniture and lighting so the finished room works as a whole.

See how those choices come together in our [Boca Raton high-rise residence](/projects/home-s/), a South Florida portfolio example.
