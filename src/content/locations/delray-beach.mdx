## Waterfront interiors that make room for the view

In this [Delray Beach waterfront estate](/projects/home-fl/), custom paneling, deep-toned wood and layered neutral seating gave the interior its own character without taking attention away from the water. The studio coordinated the interior architecture, lighting, art advisory and turnkey furnishings so the rooms felt connected.

## Designed for the way you use your home

We start with how you arrive, entertain and spend time day to day. For a waterfront home, that can mean keeping sightlines open from the entry, planning lighting around strong afternoon sun and choosing finishes that work in high-traffic rooms. For a renovation, we identify where built-in storage or a new layout will make the greatest difference before specifying finishes.
