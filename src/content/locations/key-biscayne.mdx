## Design for daily life on the island

A home can be polished without feeling delicate. We plan for the daily routines that matter: where sandy shoes go, how the dining room takes extra guests and how curtains can soften afternoon light without losing the view. We select materials by how they feel and how they will hold up in the room.

## Make the most of the home you own

For a family home, a good brief may start with storage, circulation or a bathroom that needs better lighting. For a condo, we ask which fixtures can stay and what the building allows before we design anything that changes the layout. Our [residential design service](/luxury-residential-interior-design-miami/) brings furnishings and millwork together, and independent licensed contractors handle construction as applicable.

## Arrive to a fully furnished residence

Owners who come for part of the year can review furniture plans and finishes remotely. Our [turnkey interior design service](/turnkey-interior-design-miami/) can coordinate receiving, installation and styling, subject to the scope and building access. If your residence needs furniture rather than a renovation, [Design Express](/design-express-miami/) is a more focused option.
