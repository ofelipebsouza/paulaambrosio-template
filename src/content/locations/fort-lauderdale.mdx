## Design that leaves room for the water

In a waterfront home, a floor plan should let you see the water from more than one chair. We look at the route from entry to terrace, the sightlines from the kitchen and how direct afternoon light changes a material. Low-profile seating, layered window treatments and lighting you can adjust let the interior work long after sunset.

## A practical plan for condominium installations

Before ordering, we ask about service elevators, delivery hours, insurance requirements and the dimensions of doors and corridors. For a second home, our [turnkey design service](/turnkey-interior-design-miami/) coordinates furnishing, delivery, installation and styling so each decision has an owner and a deadline.

See our [Delray Beach waterfront estate](/projects/home-fl/) for an example of how we balance layered materials with an open view.
