## Interiors that honor Mediterranean architecture

Barrel tile roofs, coral stone, arched loggias and generous courtyards are part of the architectural character of Coral Gables. Inside a Mediterranean Revival home, features such as wood-beamed ceilings, wrought iron and deep window openings deserve a design approach that works with them.

We pair natural materials, tailored millwork and layered lighting with furnishings scaled to the architecture. The goal is a home that feels connected to its history and comfortable for the way you live today.

## Our services in Coral Gables

- [Turnkey Interior Design](/turnkey-interior-design-miami/): From concept and procurement to installation and final styling, we coordinate the details of a move-in-ready home.
- [Luxury Residential Design](/luxury-residential-interior-design-miami/): Full-service design for primary residences, estates and second homes, room by room or throughout the property.
- [Custom Millwork](/millwork/): Libraries, closets, kitchens and built-ins designed to fit the proportions of your home.
- [Design Consultation](/design-consultation-miami/): A working session focused on layout, materials and finishes so you can move ahead with a clear plan.

## Neighborhoods we serve

We work throughout Coral Gables, including Gables Estates, Cocoplum, the Old Cutler corridor, the Granada and Biltmore areas, Riviera, Snapper Creek and South Gables. We also serve nearby Pinecrest and Coconut Grove.

## A seamless process for busy and remote clients

Our process gives you clear decisions and updates without asking you to be on site every week.

1. **Discovery.** We learn how you live, what you love and what the home needs.
2. **Concept.** Mood boards, layouts and 3D presentations bring the direction to life.
3. **Selection.** We curate furnishings, fabrics, lighting and finishes for your approval.
4. **Procurement and coordination.** We track orders and coordinate deliveries and trades within the agreed scope.
5. **Installation and styling.** We install and style the interior for the final handover.
