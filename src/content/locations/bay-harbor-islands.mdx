## Make a home easy to return to

A second home should feel settled the moment you arrive. We plan where bags land at the entry, whether a guest room can double as a workspace and which items should be easy to find between visits. That plan guides furniture sizes, storage and lighting rather than starting with a shopping list.

## Condos and houses call for different plans

On East Island, condo furniture deliveries need to fit the building's access, elevator and scheduling rules. We confirm those details before committing to a delivery date. For a West Island house, we look at how the entry, kitchen and living areas connect before deciding where a new furniture plan or [custom cabinetry](/millwork/) will make the biggest difference.
