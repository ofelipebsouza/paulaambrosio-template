## Play, with Purpose

A game room asks a lot of one plan: room to cue a shot, seats for the spectators, a bar that keeps the evening moving, and display for whatever the collection happens to be. We set each zone deliberately so the room stays generous even when it is full.

## The Bar Sets the Tone

Whether lacquered in turquoise or wrapped in dark wood, the bar is the social anchor of the room. Shelving and accent lighting turn bottles and glassware into the décor — the simplest way to make a leisure room feel like the best seat in the house.
