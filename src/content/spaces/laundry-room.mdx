## Designed Like a Galley

Washing, drying, sorting, folding: every task deserves its own place. Stacking the washer and dryer returns the floor plan to the room, while a deep utility sink and a continuous stone countertop create one uninterrupted work surface for treating, soaking and folding in a single move.

## Storage That Disappears

Cabinetry above and below swallows detergent, linens and the machinery of daily life; open shelving keeps the everyday essentials within arm's reach without adding visual noise. Baskets corral the loose ends. What remains on display is only what earns its place.

## Materials That Work Hard

A utility room deserves the same material attention as the rest of the home — stone that shrugs off water, millwork finished to survive humidity, hardware that feels good in the hand. Practical choices, detailed beautifully, are what separate a laundry room from a closet with appliances in it.
