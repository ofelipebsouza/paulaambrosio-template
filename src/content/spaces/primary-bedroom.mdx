## Protect the space around the bed

A room feels more restful when you can walk around the bed without weaving around furniture. Start by checking drawer and closet clearances. Select nightstands after you know the height of the mattress and where reading lights can reach.

## Plan how the room changes at night

Daytime light and reading light do different jobs. A dimmable light by the bed can be easier to use than switching on every ceiling fixture. Soft window treatments can control glare and privacy without hiding the view all day. A low cabinet can keep electronics and charging cables out of sight.
