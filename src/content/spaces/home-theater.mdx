## Dark Room, Warm Welcome

The best screening rooms are not black boxes with a screen bolted to the end. We balance absorption and reflection so dialogue stays intelligible, then layer cove and step lighting so the room still feels like part of the home between films.

## Seating Is the Architecture

Rows, lounges, loveseats: the seating plan sets sightlines, acoustics and mood at once. Deep sectional lounges invite marathon viewing; tiered rows make every seat the best seat. The screen wall — framed, slatted or stone — is the room's quiet centerpiece.
