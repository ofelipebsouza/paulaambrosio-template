## Planning the Ritual

A dressing room begins with behavior, not cabinetry: what you wear, in what order, and where the pause happens. We map hanging lengths, folded storage, shoes and accessories first — then wrap the plan in millwork so the room reads as one calm gesture instead of a grid of doors.

## Light, Inside and Out

Glass-front wardrobes, backlit displays and cove lighting turn the collection itself into the decoration. The right light flatters both the clothes and the person choosing them — which is, after all, the point of the room.
