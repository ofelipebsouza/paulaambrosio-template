## Start with the way the bathroom is used

A family bathroom needs a different plan from a compact powder room. We first look at who uses it, what needs to stay dry and where frequently used items should go. Then we decide on the vanity, shower and door clearances before selecting stone and fixtures.

## Plan the light and the surfaces together

In Coral Gables, dark framed glass separates the shower without closing off the room. The floating vanity keeps the floor visually open. A powder room can support a more dramatic finish because it does not have the same moisture and storage needs as a daily shower room.
