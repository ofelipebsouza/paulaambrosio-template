## Composition in Layers

A living room resolves from the architecture outward: the path of daylight, the anchor wall, the seating plan that lets a conversation happen across a table instead of a television. Art, textiles and objects arrive last — they are the accents, not the argument.

## Materials You Live With

Stone that takes a glass without a coaster, upholstery that forgives a Sunday, wood that warms as the light drops. We specify for the way a room is actually used, so the first year and the tenth look remarkably alike.
