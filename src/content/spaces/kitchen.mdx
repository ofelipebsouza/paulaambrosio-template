## Start with circulation

Before choosing a countertop, map the walk from the refrigerator to the sink and cooking area. Allow space for an open dishwasher or refrigerator without blocking people coming through. A counter for working should not be the same spot where every bag gets dropped.

## Give each light a job

In our [North Miami residence](/projects/home-aa/), light along the work surface makes the pale cabinetry legible while the oak introduces warmth. In Boca Raton, the recess puts frequently used items near the work area but out of the main sightline. Those ideas work at different scales because each starts with the habits of the household.
