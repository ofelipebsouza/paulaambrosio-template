Bal Harbour rewards restraint. This oceanfront residence is composed around the principles of quiet luxury: honed stone, warm oak, hand-applied plaster and a lighting system calibrated to the client's art collection.

## Scope

The studio delivered interior architecture and complete turnkey execution, including custom Italian millwork, art advisory and lighting, and white-glove staging, coordinating with the building's board requirements and delivering the residence fully installed and styled.
