## Project Description

ATLANTIC is a design development study presented entirely in photorealistic 3D render. The amenity program is visualized room by room — kitchen, lounge bar and card room, party room, men's lounge, and mail and package — so that layout, joinery and lighting are agreed while changes are still simple to make.

## Design Approach

The study follows the order in which the amenity floor is used: the kitchen and the lounge bar and card room first, then the party room and men's lounge, with the mail and package area resolved as the working support space. Rendering each area as its own set keeps the conversation specific — proportions, seating and light before anything is specified.
