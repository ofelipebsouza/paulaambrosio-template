The starting point for this penthouse at **Rosewood Residences Hillsboro Beach** was the view. The living room uses a low sofa, sheer drapery and a quiet palette so the ocean remains the focus. Round sconces and illuminated shelves give the room a warmer character after dark.

## A quieter palette for the bedrooms

The guest room pairs a curved upholstered headboard with a practical writing surface. In the primary suite, a floating wood console leaves the floor visually open, while soft ceiling lighting carries the warmth of the living room into a more private space.
