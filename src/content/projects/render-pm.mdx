## Project Description

Commercial Bar is presented in photorealistic 3D render. A green bar counter and a row of upholstered stools face illuminated bottle shelving, with lounge seating arranged in the foreground.

## Design Approach

Warm wood surfaces, dark wall finishes and green upholstery frame the bar. Planting above the shelves and linear overhead lighting add detail around the counter and seating area.
