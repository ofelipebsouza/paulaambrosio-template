## Project Description

DAFNE is presented entirely in photorealistic 3D render. The salon is resolved area by area — entry, bar and seating, color, shampoo, wash and VIP — so that the movement between client-facing and technical spaces is agreed before any construction begins.

## Design Approach

Every area was rendered as part of the same set, which makes the transitions visible: how a guest arrives, where the seating and bar sit, and how the color, shampoo and wash stations were arranged around them. The VIP room closes the sequence as the most private space of the salon, and the complete set is shown here so no area is decided in isolation.
