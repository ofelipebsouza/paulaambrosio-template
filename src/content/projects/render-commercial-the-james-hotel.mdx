## Project Description

THE JAMES HOTEL is presented in photorealistic 3D render. The corridors, shower rooms and locker areas are visualized as one continuous sequence, so lighting, surfaces and detailing are agreed across every space a guest passes through.

## Design Approach

These are the rooms guests judge quickly and remember, and the least forgiving to build, so each view answers a specific question: how the corridor reads on arrival, how the showers are compartmented, and how the lockers and changing areas perform in daily operation.
