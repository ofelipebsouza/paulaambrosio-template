A primary residence for a multigenerational family on Williams Island. The plan opens compartmentalized rooms into light-filled volumes oriented to the marina, while performance textiles and honed stone keep the interior effortlessly livable.

## Scope

Full-service interior design and interior architecture, custom themed children's suites, millwork, furnishings and procurement, and final styling, delivered as a single coordinated project from concept to installation.
