A waterfront estate on Star Island asks for interiors that hold their own against the view. The composition here is built from layered natural textures, deep-toned woods and custom millwork that frames, rather than competes with, the water.

## Scope

The studio delivered the complete interior: interior architecture, custom millwork, curated lighting, art advisory and full turnkey furnishings. Every room was resolved to the same material language, so the house reads as one continuous work from arrival to the water's edge.
