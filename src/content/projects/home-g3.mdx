A retail environment for a luggage house, designed so that merchandising, hospitality and work sit comfortably side by side inside a single clear glass box.

## The Brief

The client needed more than a shop. A showroom able to host clients, a meeting room for negotiations, a small lounge for waiting and a working office all had to live on one floor plate, and the retail side had to stay the first thing anyone reads when they walk in.

## Design Approach

Micro-cement walls and a pale terrazzo floor run uninterrupted from the storefront to the back office, removing every visual break that would otherwise chop the plan into rooms. Against that quiet surface the floating oak shelving becomes the only vertical rhythm in the space, so the display is always the loudest element and the architecture is always the calmest.

Fluted glass in slim black frames divides the meeting room, the lounge and the workspace. The partitions hold acoustics and sightlines while letting daylight travel the full depth of the floor, which keeps a deep plan from ever feeling like a corridor.

Lighting is layered for two different audiences: calibrated track spots aimed at the display wall for merchandising, and warm recessed downlights wherever people sit, talk or work. The same room therefore performs as a shop by day and as a workplace after hours.

## Scope

Interior design, space planning, custom millwork, lighting design, furniture procurement, styling and final installation by Paula Ambrosio Interiors.
